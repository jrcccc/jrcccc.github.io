About me

I'm a cosmologist and postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Theoretical Astrophysics, University of Oslo. I study the early Universe through large-scale numerical simulations, with a focus on topological defects (cosmic strings and domain walls), axions, and the gravitational waves produced by cosmological phase transitions.

A common thread in my research is high-performance computing. I develop GPU-accelerated (CUDA, multi-GPU) field-theory codes for large-scale simulations of the early Universe. Before Oslo, I was a postdoc at the University of Helsinki (2022–2025), and I did my PhD at the University of Porto (2017–2022).

Research topics

  • Topological defects

    Field-theory simulations of cosmic strings and domain walls, and calibration of analytic models of their evolution.

  • Axions & dark matter

    Axion string networks in the post-inflationary scenario, the axions they radiate, and predictions for the axion mass.

  • Gravitational waves

    Simulations of strong first-order phase transitions in the early Universe and the gravitational-wave signal they produce.

  • High-performance computing

    GPU-accelerated (CUDA, multi-GPU) codes for large-scale lattice simulations in cosmology.
